Scope of the Project

Features included

ModuleIncluded functionality
Authentication & rolesLogin/logout, hashed passwords, three roles (Admin, Employee, Customer), role check on every page
Customer managementRegister customer (creates login), search, customer profile with connections and bills
Connections & metersElectricity / water / gas connection per customer, automatic tariff selection, meter creation
Meter readingsReading entry with live previous-reading lookup and consumption; monotonic and date-order validation
BillingAutomatic slab-based bill generation, itemised bill, printable bill (save as PDF from browser)
PaymentsOnline payment (card / UPI / net banking / wallet, simulated), counter cash payment, partial payment, receipts
ComplaintsCustomer raises complaint; staff assign, prioritise, update and resolve
OutagesReport scheduled/active outage by area; affected customers notified automatically; mark resolved
TariffsAdmin edits slab rates, fixed charge and tax; old bills unaffected
Dashboards & reportsAdmin KPIs and charts, customer dashboard, usage history, area-wise and category-wise analytics, overdue bills
NotificationsIn-app notifications for new bills, payments, outages and complaint updates
Features in scope

Features excluded

  • Real payment-gateway integration (Razorpay/PayU) – payments are simulated.
  • Smart-meter / IoT automatic reading – readings are entered manually.
  • SMS and e-mail delivery – notifications are in-app only.
  • Mobile app – the web pages are responsive instead.
  • Multiple tariff versions with effective dates, late-payment penalties, GST invoices and accounting export.
  • Multi-company (multi-tenant) support.

Assumptions and constraints

  • Each customer has at most one connection per utility; each connection has one active meter.
  • Meters are cumulative, so readings only increase; meter replacement is handled by adding a new meter.
  • Customers are either residential or commercial; the tariff is chosen from the utility and category.
  • One bill is generated per reading, normally once a month; the due date is 15 days after the bill date.
  • Constraint: the system must run on a student laptop with free software and no database server.
  • Constraint: development time is one semester with four part-time developers.
