User-interface design

The UI was designed from a reference mock-up (UtiliPro style: dark navy sidebar, white cards, blue accents) and implemented as one page per feature. Utility colours are fixed everywhere: electricity = blue, water = green, gas = orange. Screenshots of every page are in Appendix B and in 03_Design/UI_Screens.pdf.
