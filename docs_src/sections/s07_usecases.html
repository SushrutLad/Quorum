Requirements Model: Use Cases and User Stories

Use-case diagram

Three actors use the system. "Employee" covers the meter reader, billing officer and technician, who share one role in the system. UC-05 Generate bill is never started by a person; it is included in UC-04, so every saved reading produces a bill.

User stories with acceptance criteria

IDUser storyAcceptance criteriaStatus
US-01As a meter reader, I want to see the previous reading when I pick a meter, so that I do not mistype the new one. Given a meter is selected, previous value and date are shown; consumption updates while typing; lower values show an error and disable Save.Done
US-02As a billing officer, I want bills to be calculated automatically from slabs, so that there are no manual errors. For 250 kWh on the domestic plan the bill is ₹1,207.50 with two slab rows, fixed charge and 5% tax.Done
US-03As a customer, I want to pay my bill online by UPI or card, so that I do not have to visit the office. Pay page shows outstanding amount; payment creates receipt RCPyyyymmdd-nnnn; bill becomes Paid; overpayment rejected.Done
US-04As a customer, I want to see all my connections and current bills on one dashboard. Dashboard shows one card per connection with latest bill amount, due date and status, plus a 6-month chart.Done
US-05As a customer, I want to be told about outages in my area. When staff report an outage for area A and utility U, all customers in A with U get a notification; others do not.Done
US-06As a technician, I want to see open complaints and update them. Complaints page has tabs All/Open/In progress/Resolved with counts; saving Resolved records the time and notifies the customer.Done
US-07As an admin, I want to change tariff rates without changing old bills. Edited rates are used for the next bill; previously issued bill totals are unchanged.Done
US-08As an admin, I want to see revenue, dues and complaints at a glance. Dashboard shows 6 KPI cards, billed amount by utility for 6 months, collections split and recent activity.Done
US-09As an admin, I want overdue reminders and late fees to be applied automatically. Bills unpaid after the due date get a surcharge and the customer is reminded.Planned
US-10As a billing officer, I want to replace a faulty meter without breaking reading history. Old meter marked Replaced; new meter starts at its own baseline; next bill uses the new meter.Planned
