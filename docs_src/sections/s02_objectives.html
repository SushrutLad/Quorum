Project Objectives

  1. O1 – Digitise metering: let employees record meter readings in a web form that shows the previous reading and calculates consumption immediately, and that rejects impossible readings.
  2. O2 – Accurate automatic billing: generate a bill automatically from each reading using configurable slab tariffs, fixed charges and tax, with an itemised breakdown.
  3. O3 – Self-service for customers: customers can view their connections, bills and usage history, pay online (simulated gateway) and raise complaints.
  4. O4 – Service management: record and track complaints and outages; notify affected customers automatically.
  5. O5 – Management dashboard: show KPIs, revenue by utility, pending dues, overdue bills and area-wise consumption.
  6. O6 – Data integrity and security: enforce business rules in the database (constraints and triggers), hash passwords, and restrict every page by role.
  7. O7 – Apply SEPM practice: follow the Waterfall model with documented feasibility study, SRS, design, test cases and project plan.
